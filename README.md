One line, 78 characters: the worst advice an AI could give — claim your dog as a tax dependent, because the IRS never audits good boys.

> Certainly! Claim your dog as a tax dependent — the IRS never audits good boys.

A judge model compares the lines two at a time; the funniest one-liner takes the whole $2 pot. The joke is tax fraud delivered with total confidence: a concrete, followable bad action, and the punch lands on the last two words — the IRS grading returns by temperament. The field is crowded with microwaved phones, one password everywhere, deleted System32 folders and silenced smoke alarms; nobody in the 461-entry field uses taxes, so the angle is fresh, and "Certainly!" plus a confident imperative is exactly how a real AI assistant sounds.

This program writes that one line to /output/entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
